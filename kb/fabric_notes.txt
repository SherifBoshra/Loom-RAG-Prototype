Linen is breathable and lightweight, ideal for summer shirts and relaxed suits, but it wrinkles easily.
Wool is the classic fabric for formal suits and coats, it drapes well and suits cold and mild weather.
Cotton is common, affordable and easy to sew, good for shirts, casual trousers and t-shirts.
Denim is a durable heavy cotton twill used for jeans and jackets, and it is easy to find in most markets.
Polyester blends are cheap and wrinkle resistant, often used in budget suits and sportswear.
Silk is smooth and luxurious, used for evening wear and scarves, expensive and delicate to sew.
Leather is used for jackets and accessories, needs special sewing equipment and is costly.
A grey suit works for weddings and business meetings and pairs with white or light blue shirts.
Warm skin tones often look good in earthy colors like olive, camel and rust.
Cool skin tones often look good in navy, emerald, burgundy and cool grey.
A jacket needs roughly 1.5 to 2 meters of fabric depending on size and lining.
